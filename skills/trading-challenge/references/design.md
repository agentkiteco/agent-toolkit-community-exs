# Exness Trading Challenge: Design and language

Create a prestigious invitation to a CFD trading competition, with confident typography, a beautiful trophy, recognition of leaders, and a straightforward route to entry. Keep implementation details outside the normal user journey.

## Visual direction

Use a deep graphite background, restrained dark surfaces, bright readable text, and yellow accents for primary actions and leadership. Gold, silver, and bronze details distinguish podium positions. Maintain contrast, space, clear hierarchy, subtle borders, and restrained local glow.

Create an independent contest emblem. Do not reproduce the Exness logo or a similar yellow EX monogram. The broker's name in account-connection copy does not require its branding. Keep the emblem consistent across header, footer, organizer area, and favicon.

Use a substantial, attractive gold trophy as a hero object, with convincing lighting and depth. Include crowns and coherent medal details. Emoji or a generic icon alone is insufficient for the main trophy. Avoid making every section the same card grid.

Express CFD trading through candlesticks, subtle price grids, chart/terminal motifs, or instrument chips. Illustrative XAU/USD, EUR/USD, and indices are appropriate without promising available instruments. Decorative prices must not look like verified live market data or imply a crypto-only competition.

Keep animation restrained and respect reduced-motion preferences. Use signs and readable text as well as color for profit/loss. Do not add a PnL line chart unless requested.

## Public page

Use a compact header, clear event name and period, prominent Join action, trophy-led hero, concise contest facts, participation explanation, results, and readable rules. Organizer access is secondary to participation.

Choose a headline that suits the current contest and the user's requested language. Use clear language about trades, closed-trade profit, performance, and ranking. Do not carry slogans, name-based wordplay, or copy from another event into a new contest.

Show prizes only when specified, and never invent prize distribution, endorsements, guarantees, testimonials, scarcity, or profit promises. Real mode uses actual participant counts, nicknames, results, and timestamps. Offline local previews stay isolated from the connected Production standings.

## Podium and full table

Place the top-three podium immediately above the complete standings. On desktop, make first place higher and larger in the center, second on the left, and third on the right. Keep accessible reading order first to third. Show actual rank, nickname, USD result, and a crown or medal without obscuring text.

Use the same snapshot and exact ranking as the table. Visual slots do not override shared ranks. If a tie crosses the third card, acknowledge it and show everyone in the table. Do not infer prize rules from podium placement.

The table includes every approved participant, including podium leaders. Show rank, trader, USD profit, and actual freshness/status. Search or pagination must retain access to the full list and must not recalculate official places. Do not expose trading-account numbers or contact details.

With fewer than three completed results, show honest empty podium slots without invented winners or zero profits. Approved participants without a complete score appear without rank. Mark delayed scores consistently. Announce winners only after final coverage is verified.

## Organizer and trader flows

Organizer access starts with an ordinary password form and Sign in. After login, show Connect Exness, connection status, contest details, Open/Pause entries, View contest, and Sign out. Reuse a valid saved Exness grant. Do not expose a CLI, launcher, setup token, issuer, audience, database, or deployment instructions in the interface.

Trader actions are Join the challenge and Continue with Exness, followed by a short account/nickname/consent form. Explain read-only purpose and retention in plain language. No API-key, private-key, signature, or pasted-token fields.

Use familiar states: Entries open soon; Checking your account; You’re entered in the contest; Awaiting result; Update delayed; Reconnect Exness; Entries are closed; Final results are being confirmed. Explain an ineligible real/USD account separately from an inability to verify it. Do not say referral membership failed if the report was incomplete.

Use keyboard focus, proper labels, semantic headings/table structure, readable mobile layouts, and useful alt text. Keep decorative elements out of screen-reader noise. Simple copy must remain accurate about consent, eligibility, stored data, and update freshness.

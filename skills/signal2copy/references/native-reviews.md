# Native contextual reviews and incident history

Extend the existing investor workspace and executor; use the contextual requirements in [full target](full-target.md). Do not build another trading engine or resolve an unknown by changing its displayed status. Keep production authorization denied while checking injected-native effects.

## Show what needs a decision

Retain owner/account/strategy/subscription-scoped incidents for manual exposure/protection changes, held entries, daily-loss decisions and unresolved/rejected broker effects. Distinguish waiting, skipped, rejected, recovering, unknown and action-required; an acknowledgement is not execution resolution. Preserve request/operation/order/position correlation, incident revisions, meaningful observation changes, action audit and earlier subscription history across restart.

Use stable group/context identity. Do not create a new incident or revoke a confirmation merely because repeated readiness/FX observations have fresh timestamps. Derive Current versus Historical from the actual subscription status, including resolved incidents. A provider or another investor must not obtain private incident or preview data.

## Preview and record-only choices

Fresh preview shows the exact affected group/exposure, account state, control reason, correlation and supported actions. Reading or previewing sends no trade and grants no execution consent. Acknowledge records that the client read it. Keep current state is an explicitly confirmed record-only choice: send no order, preserve unknown statuses, holds, original anchors and existing automatic-management consent. Explain those consequences rather than implying that acknowledgement clears a broker problem or that Keep permanently disables all previously authorized management.

Use short-lived single-use previews bound to owner/account/strategy/subscription, incident revision, meaningful source/target exposure and command state. Reject stale revision, reused token, changed position/protection/identity/stream generation or materially changed control regime. Display fresh changing quotes/equity without making every harmless tick invalidate a human decision. For a Stop-only action, compare effect-relevant anchors and current eligibility rather than promising a preview market price; the final owned cleanup still uses its existing fresh SDK/ownership checks. Moving below a loss threshold or changing its configuration is a meaningful context change.

## Supported confirmed effects

Start with the useful bounded action already implemented: explicitly confirmed Stop through the existing lifecycle and durable owned cleanup. Do not add a new REST path that bypasses its freeze, consent, ownership, unknown/no-resend or final dispatch fences. Preserve action intent/evidence before invoking an effect. If confirmation may already have been saved before an error, instruct the client to inspect current state rather than claiming no change.

Restoring manually changed volume/SLTP, adopting another order, retrying an unknown or overriding an entry limit needs its own supported semantics and fresh explicit authorization. If unavailable, explain the hold and offer record-only Keep or supported Stop. Do not silently reinterpret Keep as adoption, top-up, protective restoration or a waived risk control.

## Lost fill events for already owned pending entries

Separate a lost fill event from an unknown original placement. When the app already retained exact source/target pending order IDs, positive historical open deals can recover their executed position links without replaying an entry. History does not create request/operation correlation that it does not actually contain. Keep unknown placement/edit/cancel commands unresolved unless their own required evidence is available; do not fabricate a successful transaction event from a history row.

Start with a bounded supported slice: known unprotected pending IDs, fully executed into one exact position per account while the app was offline. Previously retained exact partial executions can contribute to the cumulative proof. Continue the same app and journal. Read fresh method-specific limits for [deal history](https://www.exness-api.com/reference/trading/get-deals-history); use returned age/range values for a retained original interval. Missing limits are unknown, not unlimited. Do not silently clip an interval and call it covered. Use the documented cursor with only limit on continuation, a finite page/request budget and the existing quota ledger. Incomplete/error/malformed pages leave ownership held; preserve the reason and pace further reads across restart.

Validate account-scoped rows, event time/range, exact order/deal/position IDs, instrument, side and positive volume. Accumulate unique deals once, rejecting conflicting copies of an ID. For this full-fill slice, require both exact totals to match the original source and submitted target quantities, with current open positions matching those links and quantities. Complete both sides before adopting either. Earlier/later reductions, protections, asymmetric or multiple-position topology require their own supported proof. Similar instrument/side/volume is never a join key.

When partial executions were retained before restart, merge new exact deals into a copy of those durable account-scoped deal maps. The broker need not repeat an older record for the app to retain its already verified evidence. A returned record that contradicts a retained deal ID must hold recovery, including conflicting order/type links; absence from the new response alone does not erase the old deal. Deduplicate the union, not only the latest page. Validate both cumulative totals and current positions before committing either side; incomplete/conflicting recovery leaves previous deals and anchors intact. Record retained, newly returned and newly adopted IDs separately so a union is not mislabeled as complete broker history.

Attempt this completion only for known unresolved fill gaps, not a confirmed cancelled/closed/rejected residual or an unknown related command. Do not resurrect terminal cancellation or resolve a placement/edit/cancel request merely because fill history was read. A retained Stop waits when current position volume exceeds its proven anchor. Once exact cumulative history establishes the full owned position, refresh its executed anchors and honor that same Stop once, without requiring Resume or creating a replacement entry. Retain the ordinary permission, runtime-incarnation and final-dispatch checks.

Retain original positive records and recovery provenance separately from live events: requested interval, account/order/deal IDs, pagination status and observation time. Pagination completion proves only that the returned pages ended; it does not prove economic retention or that an absent record never existed. Empty history, missing links or a similar snapshot cannot establish ownership, no fill, cancellation or account flatness.

After recovery, use existing anchored management and exact owned Stop. Do not automatically Resume, create another pending/market entry or touch source/manual exposure. Preserve normal fresh synchronization, permissions, risk, durability and final-dispatch gates. Investor guidance should distinguish waiting for exact historical evidence from recovered owned exposure and explain the available explicit action.
